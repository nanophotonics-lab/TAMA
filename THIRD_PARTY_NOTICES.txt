The upstream-derived material and adjoint tests in tests/meep/test_upstream_*.py
adapt Meep v1.34.0 tests and document comparisons with its material implementation,
at commit 3e7b7fee0da4a9b91b07acd9f71d35d674396563. Meep copyright belongs to
Massachusetts Institute of Technology; GPL-2.0-or-later notices and pinned source
references are retained in the adapted files. The nondispersive analytic-wave
adaptation removes the original Lorentz response and computes new reference values.
The adjoint ports adapt test_adjoint_solver.py and the physical gradient cases
in test_adjoint_jax.py. Their test files document temporal and modal adaptations.
